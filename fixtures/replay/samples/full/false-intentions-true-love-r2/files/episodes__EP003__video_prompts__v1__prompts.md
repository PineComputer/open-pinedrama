Photorealistic live-action short drama with cinematic lighting. Each character keeps the face and the outfit with its main colour from their reference image throughout. Characters, props and environment share one world with matched lighting. Each line ends naturally with breathing room and is never cut off. Authentic performances with micro-expressions. No BGM; generate only dialogue, environmental sounds and action sounds. No subtitles.

added or missing characters, spatial jumps. malformed hands and fingers. subtitles or garbled text. Dialogue from the wrong character, multiple people speaking at once, repeated or missing lines, lip-sync errors and audio-visual desynchronization. The raw reference images (character sheets, setting plates) appearing as a frame of the video, and starting on a still frame of the setting reference. A fast zoom-in at the start, a fast pull-back at the end, unmotivated whip-pans, tilts, zooms and 360-degree orbits. Axis-crossing, jump cuts, frame skips, flicker and clipping. Black-and-white, monochrome, grayscale, desaturated visuals; hand-drawn, sketch, line art, illustration, comics, animation; storyboard frames, rough sketches, panel borders, gutters and grid layouts.

Shot 3 (6-10s): [Medium close-up, slow push in on Ji Ran, eye level, from the pin-up wall side angled toward the desk, clean single framed on face and shoulders] Ji Ran lets her eyes travel slowly from Lin Qingyu's face down his soaked body. Shot 5 (14-17s): [Medium shot, follow Xu Banxia from the desk corner to the walnut door, eye level, from the pin-up wall side] Xu Banxia presses her lips together to hold in a laugh, walks from the desk corner past Ji Ran and Lin Qingyu, steps out through the walnut door and pulls it shut behind her. <Door clicks shut>
Shot 6 (17-20s): [Medium shot, follow Ji Ran around Lin Qingyu, eye level, camera on the pin-up wall side] Ji Ran folds her arms and walks slowly around Lin Qingyu, inspecting him, ending in front of him on the desk side, facing him, while he stands just inside the closed door. <Stiletto heels click on the oak floor>
Shot 7 (20-23s): [Close-up insert, locked-off camera, eye level, on Lin Qingyu's stomach] Ji Ran's finger pokes Lin Qingyu's abdomen through the wet white T-shirt, the fabric denting with each poke. Dialogue (Ji Ran, in Chinese, deadpan counting): {一、二、三、四。 } <Cartoon boing with each poke>
Shot 8 (23-25s): [Medium close-up, handheld, low angle on Lin Qingyu, dirty single with Ji Ran's shoulder at the frame edge] Lin Qingyu stands stiffly with his arms at his sides, a bead of sweat sliding down his forehead, eyes darting down to her hand. Dialogue (Ji Ran, in Chinese, crisp): {你叫什么名字？}
Shot 9 (25-27s): [Medium shot, over-the-shoulder from behind Ji Ran, eye level, angled toward the walnut door] Over Ji Ran's shoulder, Lin Qingyu swallows, a shy flush on his face. Dialogue (Lin Qingyu, in Chinese, stammering): {林、林青羽。}
At the end: Camera on the front-wall side, by the pin-up wall, facing the walnut desk and the walnut door on the right wall. Lin Qingyu stands on the pale oak floor just inside the closed walnut door, soaked, sweating, facing the desk. Ji Ran stands in front of him on the desk side, arms folded, facing him. Xu Banxia has left through the walnut door, which is shut. The walnut desk and black leather chair stay in front of the floor-to-ceiling windows.
